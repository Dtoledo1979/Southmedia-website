FAQ

Christmas party questions.

How far in advance should we book a Christmas party DJ?

As early as possible. Late November and the first three weeks of December are the busiest dates of the year, especially Fridays and Saturdays. Enquire now and we'll confirm availability within 48 hours.

How much does a Christmas party DJ cost?

It depends on the length of the event, the size of the venue and how much sound, lighting and production you need. Every party is quoted individually — send us your details and you'll get a clear proposal with everything included, quoted + GST.

Can you play music for a mixed-age team?

Yes. Our DJs play open format — from relaxed background music during dinner to throwbacks, current hits and Latin, reading the room as the night goes. You can also send requests and a do-not-play list.

Do you provide a microphone for speeches and awards?

Yes. Let us know about speeches, prize-giving or announcements when you enquire and we'll build them into the run sheet.

Can you do outdoor or venue-hire events?

Yes — we bring the sound and lighting to your venue, indoors or outdoors. Tell us where you're holding the party and we'll advise on setup and power.

Do you travel outside Christchurch?

Yes. We're based in Christchurch and perform across Canterbury and New Zealand, including Wellington, Queenstown, Dunedin and Nelson.
