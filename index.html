About South Media Group

Ten years. Two DJs. One standard that hasn't changed.

South Media Group was built by DJ D'Latino and DJ MJ around a simple idea: entertainment should connect people, not just play in the background. Today that means event production, DJ entertainment, media and cultural programming for corporate clients, weddings, nightlife venues and community organisations across New Zealand.
